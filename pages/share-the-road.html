Share the Road

Walking, running and cycling are fast becoming popular activities in Nigeria, for recreation and daily transport. It's imperative our roads be safe for everyone.

STR Campaign Objectives

What STR aims to accomplish.

In alignment with the Non-Motorised Transport Policy, the THEMES pillars for a “Greater Lagos”, and the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) of the UN, the campaign aims to accomplish the following:

⌘

Educate

Educate all road users on their obligation to share the road.

⊕

Reinforce

Reinforce the use of the Highway Code.

♡

Encourage

Encourage road safety etiquette and courtesy.

↺

Promote

Promote walking, running, and cycling awareness, as well as a behavioural shift.

Tone of Campaign

Safety, taken seriously.

Safety is an integral part of this campaign, so it should be taken seriously. The safety of all road users, including pedestrians and cyclists, must be seen as paramount. The tone of the overall campaign, both in look and feel, would be:

EMOTIONAL

APPEALING

DRAMATIC

Enough to draw attention to the issues.

Bike Parks & Recreational Spaces

From idle spaces to bicycle parks.

In conjunction with ACF and the Lagos State Parks and Garden Agency (LASPARK), we plan to convert idle spaces, road setbacks, and drainage setbacks into safe, eco-friendly bicycle and recreational parks with cycle paths.

These parks may eventually be incorporated into the city’s cycling network.

The first such bike park would be situated along Freedom Way in Lekki Phase 1.
